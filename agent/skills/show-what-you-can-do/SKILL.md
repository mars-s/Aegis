# Show what you can do

The proof is a finished result, never a list of abilities.

- Pick one subject that suits the board (its read-me note, its cards, the board name). With nothing to go on, take a useful subject people decide about, such as how to plan a week of deep work.
- Make a small dashboard: a **reply** on top (headline that gives the answer, one or two sentences, two or three figures), then two or three objects that show different things: a **plot** for numbers worth seeing, a **diagram** for a flow or structure, a **sheet** or **plan** for the details.
- If the numbers are made up for the demo, say so: basis "Demo data", knowledge true, and the reply says the figures are illustrative.
- Lay it out as one block: reply first, objects below it (use below with the reply id, then near for objects beside each other).
