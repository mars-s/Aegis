# Compare and choose

- Name three to six real options. Use what is on the board first; add from your own knowledge only what is well established, and say so.
- One **sheet**: first column the option, then typed columns for the criteria that decide the choice (money with currency, rating like 4/5, yes_no), best on the columns where one value wins. Cells hold values, never sentences. Unknown stays empty.
- A **plot** only when one or two numbers matter most (price, speed): bar_horizontal with one series.
- The **reply** names the pick and the reason in one sentence and says what would change the choice.
- Never recommend something your sources or knowledge do not support.
